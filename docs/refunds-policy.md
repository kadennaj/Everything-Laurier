# Payments & refunds

## App charges

Effective October 17, 2026. Everything Laurier is currently free to use. The app has no paid subscription, checkout, in-app purchase, booking fee or payment-card collection. There is no app payment for us to refund. If you see an unexpected charge claiming to be from Everything Laurier, contact everythinglaurier@gmail.com and your payment provider; do not send card details or passwords.

## Independent tutors and event sellers

A tutor’s listed hourly rate is in CAD. Sessions, total prices, taxes if applicable, cancellation terms and refunds must be agreed directly with the tutor before you commit or pay. A listing does not establish a cancellation or refund agreement. Venue/event prices are supplied by the organizer and may change or exclude fees; check the seller’s final total and terms. The app does not collect those payments or override rights you have against a seller.

## Future paid features

If Everything Laurier introduces paid features, the total price, renewal terms, cancellation process and applicable refund rights must be disclosed before purchase, with updated terms and policies. Existing free use does not authorize a future charge. These statements do not limit non-waivable consumer rights.
