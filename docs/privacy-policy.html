Privacy policy

About this policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Version 2026-10-16

Everything Laurier is an independently operated student app for life around Laurier Waterloo, with an information and support website. It is not an official Wilfrid Laurier University service. The operator is responsible for the personal information described here. For access, correction, deletion, complaints, or any privacy question, contact everythinglaurier@gmail.com.

Accounts and student profiles

You can browse public campus information and use the map without an account. Signing in uses your email address to deliver a one-time verification code. Supabase maintains an account identifier, verification status, session tokens, and ordinary connection/security information such as IP address and user agent. Resend processes the recipient email, email content, and delivery information to deliver sign-in messages. We do not use these messages for marketing. Email verification confirms control of an inbox, not current university enrolment.

Student profiles contain the name and program you enter, and an optional year and introduction. Profiles are private by default. If you enable discovery with a verified email, your name, program, optional year and introduction, and verified email become visible to other signed-in users. Opted-in profiles and edits publish automatically unless restricted by the operator. You can turn discovery off in Your space → Edit your profile. Copies already received by others cannot be recalled.

Program searches in the student directory and course searches in the tutor directory are sent to Supabase to return matching results. Requests may appear in ordinary operational logs. We do not maintain a separate personal search-history feature.

Campus maps and optional location

The map provides building and room search, floor plans, entrances, stair and elevator markers, and estimated indoor/outdoor directions. Map searches, chosen places, route endpoints, routes, and manually selected indoor starting points are processed on your device and held in memory. They are not uploaded to our account database or stored as a location history.

If you choose a live-location control, we request foreground location permission. GPS coordinates, timestamp, accuracy, and available compass heading are used on your device to show a blue dot, orient the map, and update directions. Native location subscriptions stop when the app leaves the foreground or the map closes. Browser location subscriptions pause when the map document is hidden and stop when you turn location off or close the map. We do not request background location. Your operating system or browser supplies these readings under its own settings and privacy terms.

GPS does not identify a reliable indoor room or floor. An indoor starting point is chosen manually and labelled accordingly. You can use the map without granting location, stop live location, and change permission in device or browser settings. Map routes, travel times, entrances, and accessibility details are estimates from source plans and mapped paths, not surveyed guarantees or emergency directions.

A shared map link can include the selected building, floor, and room. Opening or reloading such a link on the web sends that URL to the web host and it may appear in ordinary request logs. The link does not include your live GPS coordinates.

The renderer and map data are included with the app; source floor images load from bundled assets on native devices or our web app's host. We do not send your coordinates to a commercial routing provider. Choosing an external maps link opens that provider with the selected destination or place and shares ordinary connection details; any location access you grant there is controlled by that provider.

Tutor applications and transcript images

Applying to tutor is optional and requires a signed-in, verified account. Each application is for one course and includes your name, verified contact email, course code and title, completed term, tutoring approach, optional relevant experience, general availability and meeting format, proposed hourly rate, consent version, submission time, status, and review decision/note.

We also collect the transcript image you deliberately choose and submit, as evidence that you completed the specific course. It may contain sensitive educational and identifying information. Before choosing it, crop or redact student numbers, addresses, unrelated courses, unrelated grades, and other unnecessary information. Only your name, the relevant course, term, and completion/grade evidence are needed. Do not upload someone else's transcript. Images are resized and re-encoded as JPEG before upload; do not rely on processing to remove visible private information.

Choosing an image opens the system image picker. We do not browse your full photo library, access your camera, or request microphone access for this feature. A prepared image may be temporarily held in memory or device cache for preview and upload. Your original image stays in your own photo library or files; deleting an application does not delete your original.

Application answers and transcripts are stored privately in Supabase. Access controls permit the applicant to manage their own application and permit the designated Everything Laurier owner to review applications and images. Ordinary students and guests cannot read another applicant's transcript, private answers, or review notes. Infrastructure administrators and service providers may access information as necessary to operate, secure, or support the service. Transcript review links are short-lived, expire after one minute, and must not be shared.

There is no automatic tutor approval, AI transcript analysis, or sale of transcripts. The owner manually checks evidence and decides whether to approve the specific course. You expressly consent before submission to private review and, only after approval, to sharing your name, verified email, course code/title, tutoring approach, availability, and rate with signed-in users. The transcript, completed term, separate experience answer, and review note do not appear in your listing. Course evidence review is not a guarantee of teaching quality, identity, results, or university endorsement.

Tutors and students arrange sessions and any payment directly outside the app. The app does not process tutor payments, collect payment-card details, or offer in-app messaging. Information you send through email or another service is subject to that service's terms. Tutors must respect academic integrity and may not offer to complete graded work for another student.

Withdraw an application from Academic help → Tutoring support → My applications to remove its listing and request removal of its uploaded image. You can also contact support to correct information or ask about a decision; a corrected course application may require a new review. Previously copied listing/contact details cannot be recalled.

Reports, blocks, and owner review

Venue line reports privately store your account identifier, selected venue, wait estimate, nightlife date, and submission time. Other users see aggregate venue estimates rather than individual reporters. A chosen venue is not a GPS reading.

Content reports store your account identifier, a reported profile, venue post, or tutor listing, a selected reason, submission time, review status, and resolution time. These records help the owner investigate concerns and remove content or restrict profiles/listings when appropriate. Owner review actions record the reviewing account, linked application/report, action, and time for accountability. The review dashboard is restricted by server-side authorization; merely knowing its address does not grant access. It is not a public complaint feed.

Profile blocks store the account identifiers involved and a snapshot of the blocked profile's display name. They hide the two students' directory profiles from each other. Blocking does not recall information already shared, prevent external email, or necessarily hide a separately approved tutor listing. You can report tutor listings separately. You can also hide individual posts or venue feeds on your device. These local choices do not remove content for everyone.

Device storage and other services

Bookmarks, hidden posts, hidden venue feeds, and the nightlife 19-or-older choice remain in local device/browser storage. We do not collect your birth date. A pending sign-in request stores your email and resend timer locally. Native sign-in credentials use secure device credential storage; web sessions use browser storage. Local bookmarks and feed preferences are not uploaded to our account database.

Supabase processes authentication, profiles, applications, transcript storage, reports, and server functions. The current database is hosted in US West; information can be processed outside Canada, including in the United States, under applicable foreign laws. Resend provides sign-in delivery. We use service providers to operate the service, not to sell applicants' educational records.

Apify retrieves selected venues' public Instagram posts for a shared feed. We do not send your account, private application, transcript, device location, profile, or line report to Apify. Selected imports publish automatically after source/link checks; this is not AI screening or manual approval of every venue post. You can report posts and the owner can remove them. Displaying Instagram-hosted media requests the image from its hosting service, which receives IP address and ordinary connection details, subject to Meta's privacy terms.

The information website is hosted through Sites on Cloudflare infrastructure. Our website and web-app hosts process ordinary web requests to deliver and secure pages and assets. The information website has no account forms, advertising analytics, or tracking cookies. The app uses essential account storage as explained above. Opening university resources, venue pages, Instagram, external maps, or email takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices. Emailing support shares your address, message, and any attachments with the operator's inbox and email provider.

We do not sell your personal information, use advertising identifiers or advertising tracking, or send your account data, transcripts, or location to third-party AI or language models. The app does not request contacts, Bluetooth, microphone, camera, Face ID, or background-location permission. Optional foreground location and deliberately selected transcript images are used only for the features described above.

Retention and security

Account and uploaded data travel over HTTPS. Database authorization and private-storage policies restrict sensitive records. Owner access is allowlisted against a verified account and a still-active server session. Expiring image links reduce exposure but cannot prevent copying by someone legitimately viewing an image. No system can eliminate every security risk. Contact us promptly if you believe information has been exposed.

Accounts and student profiles remain until account deletion. Approved tutor listing/application records remain while the listing is active, so we can administer the course approval. Transcript images are scheduled for removal after 30 days from review, after 90 days if still pending, or after one day for an abandoned draft. A daily cleanup processes eligible files; allow up to an additional 24 hours during ordinary operation. A pending application without retained evidence expires and may be resubmitted. Approval does not require us to keep a transcript indefinitely.

Withdrawal immediately removes a listing and attempts to remove its transcript; the daily cleanup retries any remaining withdrawn files. Rejected and withdrawn application records and their linked review logs are removed after their retention period of 90 days. Abandoned draft records expire after one day once their files are removed. An approved listing may remain after its transcript image expires. You may request earlier deletion or delete your account.

Line reports stop contributing to estimates after 90 minutes. Reports older than the seven-day nightlife retention window are removed by a cleanup scheduled every five minutes. Moderation reports and profile blocks are removed from the live database when the associated accounts are deleted; reports about deleted posts or tutor applications are removed when their targets are deleted. Review logs linked to a deleted application/report are also removed. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request, handle follow-up concerns, and meet applicable obligations; contact us to request its deletion.

Local preferences last until you change/clear them, remove app data, or delete your account on that device. Limited service-provider operational/security logs and backups may remain after live deletion according to provider settings and contractual retention. They are protected and expire through provider processes; they are not used to recreate deleted accounts for ordinary app use. We will assess any applicable legal preservation requirement and explain limits on a particular request.

Your choices, access, and account deletion

You can explore as a guest; decline or revoke location; choose which transcript image to submit; withdraw tutor consent/listings; turn profile discovery off; edit your profile; block profiles; hide feeds; and report content. The app's requested permissions can be managed in device/browser settings. Declining optional permissions does not block general campus browsing.

To permanently delete your account, open Your space → Delete account and confirm. The server verifies your current account and session, revokes sessions, removes your uploaded transcript files through the storage service, and deletes your live authentication account. Related student profiles, tutor applications/listings, line reports, linked content reports, blocks, and linked review logs are removed. Successful deletion also clears this device's saved items and preferences. If storage cleanup or deletion cannot finish, the app reports a failure instead of claiming success; sign in again and retry or contact support. Account deletion is permanent.

Other devices' local preferences must be cleared separately. Your original transcript image and information copied by recipients are outside our live database deletion process. If you cannot access the app, or want access, correction, a review of an application decision, or deletion assistance, email everythinglaurier@gmail.com. We may need to verify ownership before acting. We will respond to privacy requests and complaints within applicable legal time limits, explain any lawful exception, and describe further complaint options, including the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada where applicable.

Age-appropriate use and policy changes

This service is intended for university students. Do not submit a child's records or another person's educational information. Venue age requirements and alcohol laws apply independently of app access. The app is not an emergency service.

We update the effective date when our practices change. If a new use requires consent, we will request it before that use begins. The current policy is available inside the app and at https://everythinglaurier.ca/privacy/.
