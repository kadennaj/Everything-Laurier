October 16, 2026. Implementation commit `5b67481` was prepared on `feature/map-tutoring-owner-dashboard` from base main commit `9783e70` and merged into `main` at the owner's request. Map source was extracted from `origin/feature/indoor-campus-map` at `a9afe74`. The integrated app has not been submitted to an app store.

## Implemented

- `/map` integrates the real campus renderer and all 28 mapped floor models. Building transitions, connected floor views, room search, fastest/sheltered/avoid-stairs directions, manual indoor starting points, optional live outdoor GPS/heading, source-plan comparison, and 2D/3D/stack controls are retained. The app supplies navigation and branding. There is no localhost dependency in the built renderer. Map assets have no external font/CDN dependency. Floor images load on demand from web/native assets. Native uses foreground location and a narrow WebView bridge.
- Academic help → Tutoring support contains an approved course tutor directory, application form and image selection/preparation, explicit review/publication consent, status and withdrawal. Pending/draft/rejected transcripts and private answers never become directory fields.
- `/owner` is authorized by a verified server-side owner allowlist and live session. It reviews applications and reported profiles, venue posts and tutor listings. An ordinary account cannot access the queue or grant itself owner status. Decisions and logs are saved atomically.
- The combined schema and incremental migrations are committed with the app. Supabase schema, private bucket, owner allowlist, cleanup jobs, and updated deletion function are deployed to project `ekptnmermhvtrdqpjwwa`. The owner allowlist contains one account, resolved from the explicitly authorized verified email `everythinglaurier@gmail.com`.
- The policy is canonical Markdown, generated JSON for the full in-app policy, and standalone HTML. The public privacy/support/community pages are published as Sites version 6, source commit `686b3560309fd6d6bcf5457592a7d5b5a53324f4`, deployment `appgdep_6ad28edec4688191820cffc32ccae38f`. Public policy: https://everythinglaurier.ca/privacy/.

## Privacy audit scope

`scripts/audit-privacy.mjs` reads the first-party source/build/backend/test files listed with line counts and SHA-256 hashes in `privacy-review-inventory.csv`, checks for client private credentials/public transcript links, and checks the permission/policy/map-font configuration. The initial scan covered 143 files / 13,636 lines. This automated scan is supplemented by a manual data-flow review of authentication/session storage, profile publication, directory searches, line reports, moderation and blocks, tutor consent/uploads/review, retention/deletion, map sensors, external navigation, vendor imports and app configuration. It is not a line-by-line audit of vendor source, hosted provider internals, or an independent penetration test.

The policy now explicitly covers educational records, course-specific manual decisions, visible listing fields, private owner access, foreground GPS/compass, manual indoor location, shared-link URLs and web request logs, selected-image processing, providers/outside-Canada processing, image retention, withdrawal, deletion, and access/complaint rights. The old blanket claim that the app never requests photos/location has been removed. The full policy is readable in-app without relying on a possibly stale website.

## Remaining launch checks and limits

- Have a qualified Canadian privacy/legal professional review the operator's identity, actual business practices, terms and privacy notice. Accurate code and a revised policy cannot guarantee protection from lawsuits or legal compliance in every situation.
- Verify actual Supabase/Resend/hosting backup and log retention against operational settings. The policy states those limits instead of inventing a guaranteed erasure date.
- Verify the owner sign-in and actual transcript review on the owner's account, and test native foreground GPS, compass, selected-image picker, signed image display and account deletion on iOS/Android hardware using disposable accounts. Browser and bundle checks cannot establish those hardware outcomes.
- Confirm distribution rights for the supplied floor plans, photos, directories and venue media before publishing the integrated app. Existing attribution and source-owner rights are preserved. Campus routes, doors, lifts and accessible paths remain provisional and require campus validation.
- Existing automatic profile/venue publishing is preserved. Adding a report dashboard is not automated objectionable-content screening; actual moderation operation and the previously documented App Review guideline 1.2 filtering gap still need release review.
- Dependency audit after compatible patch updates and remediation still reports **28 advisories (18 high, 10 moderate; zero critical)** in the existing Expo/React Native toolchain graph. The shell-quote critical issue and source-map-js patch were updated. No forced Expo/React Native major downgrade was applied. Review upstream fixes before release; `npm audit` counts are not a legal or privacy compliance certification.
- Supabase advisors report only the two pre-existing warnings: [pg_net extension in public](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/database-linter?lint=0014_extension_in_public) and [leaked-password protection disabled](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/password-security#password-strength-and-leaked-password-protection). The app uses email OTP. Newly introduced private tables have explicit deny policies and no direct client grants.

Reference principles used for the policy review: [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada — PIPEDA fair information principles](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/p_principle/).
