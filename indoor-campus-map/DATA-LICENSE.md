# Map data attribution

The campus geometry and pedestrian network in `src/data/campus.json` are derived from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, retrieved October 12, 2026 (source snapshot: `source-data/campus-2026-10-12.osm`).

OpenStreetMap data is available under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Attribution and licensing guidance: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Database licence obligations remain separate from the repository's code licence. The map displays attribution; preserve it in future integrations.

User-supplied blueprint reference PDFs, library screenshots, and derived crops retain the rights of their respective owners. Their delivery for this prototype and inclusion in this repository do not grant an open licence to the underlying reference material.

Library reference images under `public/blueprints/library/` come from [Laurier Library’s Waterloo floor plans](https://library.wlu.ca/locations/waterloo), matching the user’s screenshots. These images retain their source owners’ rights; no open licence is asserted.

Indoor trace definitions are provisional hand-authored models based on those plans, not an official Laurier navigation dataset.

The Science reference raster and labeled SVG under `public/blueprints/science/` derive from the user-supplied `science floorplan.pdf`, a flattened Science Building Floor Plan from page 47 of a 2015 conference program. These local reference derivatives retain the source owner's rights. The labels are provisional visual transcriptions, with uncertainty explicitly recorded; they are not a current official room inventory.

The Science 3D model and corridor graph are hand traces of that same historical drawing. Entrance and stairwell positions reflect the source; present-day access and floor continuity have not been surveyed. Ambiguous room identifiers are excluded from directions.


The 22 HEIC floorplan photographs supplied in `NEW FLOORPLANS`, their local WebP copies and crops under `public/blueprints/new-floorplans/`, and the gallery retain their source owners' rights. The accompanying Arts/Concourse, Peters, Schlegel and DAWB geometry is a provisional hand trace. No open licence or current indoor-access certification is asserted for these photos or derivatives.
